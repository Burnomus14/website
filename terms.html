Wrong, faulty or not as described

If your item arrives damaged, is not the item you ordered, or is clearly not as described:

  • We will check it with the seller and, where the problem is confirmed, arrange a replacement, a repair, or a refund of that item.
  • Keep the item, its packaging and any tags as they were received until we have replied.

Reporting quickly helps us fix things fast. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under Kenyan consumer protection law, including rights about goods that are faulty or not as described.
