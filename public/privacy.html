THE COLLECTION / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information. Effective 2 November 2026.

In short:

We collect only what we need to run your account and deliver your orders. We never see your M-Pesa PIN or your password. We do not sell your information. Sellers and delivery partners see only what they need to fulfil your order.

SECTION 01

Who we are

The Collection, trading as schngor ("we", "us"), runs an online marketplace in Kenya where members can browse, buy and sell shoes and clothing. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

SECTION 02

What we collect

  • Account details: your first name, last name, username and email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase sign-in. We never see or store it.
  • Delivery details: the recipient name, phone number, area or county, city, street and building or directions you save or enter at checkout.
  • Orders and payments: the items you buy, amounts, order and tracking numbers, order status, the M-Pesa phone number you pay with, and the M-Pesa transaction reference. We never receive your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Basket and saved pieces: items you add to your basket or save.
  • Seller information: if you apply to sell or sell with us, your application note, product listings and the photos you upload.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic device and browser details, which we use for security, to prevent abuse and to fix errors.

SECTION 03

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To process payments, confirm orders and deliver them.
  • To show you your orders, updates, basket and saved pieces.
  • To let sellers and delivery partners fulfil the orders you place.
  • To resolve problems, answer questions and keep our records.
  • To protect the site against fraud, abuse and security threats.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

SECTION 04

Who we share it with

  • Sellers: for an order containing their item, the seller sees your name, phone number and delivery area so they can prepare and ship it.
  • Delivery partners: the shipper assigned to your area sees the recipient name, phone number, address and the items to deliver.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa): processes your payment using your M-Pesa phone number and the amount.
  • Google Firebase: provides sign-in and stores our database (accounts, orders and listings).
  • Cloudinary: stores and serves product photos.
  • Render: hosts the website and servers.
  • WhatsApp: if you tap a WhatsApp button, your phone opens WhatsApp with a message containing your order details, which you can edit before sending.
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Kenya. We use them because they provide the security and reliability a small shop cannot build alone.

SECTION 05

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

  • One optional visit cookie (sch_vid): if you tap Accept on the banner, we set one first-party cookie holding a random code and the date it was first set. It lasts one year. We use it only to count visits accurately, see returning visitors and learn which pieces people look at and where checkout stops. It holds nothing about who you are, and it is not shared with anyone else.
  • If you tap No thanks: no cookie is set. We still count visits in a way that cannot recognise you: a one-way code made from your connection and browser that changes every day. We do not store your IP address for this.
  • Browser storage: your browser also stores small items we need to keep you signed in and to finish sign-up and checkout, and to remember your cookie choice. These are not used to track you.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The banner will then ask again.

SECTION 06

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. We keep order and payment records for as long as we need them for our accounts, to handle disputes, and to meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove what identifies you.

SECTION 07

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it.

You can update your name, username and addresses yourself from your account page. For anything else, contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

SECTION 08

How we protect it

Connections to the site are encrypted. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase. Sign-in and password-reset attempts are rate limited, access to admin, seller and delivery tools is restricted by role, and payment confirmations are checked to avoid duplicate charges. No online service is completely risk free, but we work to keep your information safe.

SECTION 09

Children

The Collection is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

SECTION 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell you on the site.

SECTION 11

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, message us on WhatsApp: our WhatsApp contact. Please include the email address on your account so we can find your information.
